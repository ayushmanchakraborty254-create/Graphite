# Privacy Policy for Graphite

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

Graphite ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Graphite handles your information when you use our digital notebook, handwriting, and PDF annotation application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Graphite is designed with a **privacy-first, local-first** architecture. Your notes, sketches, audio clips, and documents remain primarily stored locally on your device.

* **Account Information:** If you sign in via Google Sign-In or Supabase, we access basic profile details (such as your email address and display name) solely to authenticate your identity across your devices.
* **Notes, Handwriting & Documents:** All notebooks, handwriting strokes, imported PDFs, audio recordings, and text annotations are created and processed on your device.
* **Voice-to-Text & Transcription:** Voice recordings transcribed locally using Android's speech recognizer or cloud AI services (Google Gemini / Whisper) are processed solely to convert your spoken words into text in your notebook. Audio is not used for advertising or model training.

---

### 2. Google Drive Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Graphite's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements:

* **Scope Used:** `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` (Minimum required scope).
* **Usage:** Graphite only accesses, creates, and modifies specific files that you explicitly choose to back up or restore using Graphite.
* **No Unauthorized Access:** Graphite **cannot and does not** read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
* **No Sharing or Selling:** Your Google Drive data is never transferred to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security

* **Local Storage:** Your notes and settings are stored securely within the app's sandboxed storage on your device.
* **Cloud Sync:** When you enable cloud sync, communication with cloud services (Supabase / Google Drive) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
* **No Third-Party Analytics / Trackers:** Graphite does not include third-party advertising SDKs or invasive tracking networks.

---

### 4. Your Rights and Data Control

* You can export, back up, or delete your notes at any time directly within the app.
* You can disconnect your Google account or revoke Graphite's access at any time from your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
* Disconnecting an account does not delete your locally stored notes.
